# Question sheet

Paste one block at a time. Never two. Wait for the answer, then paste the next.

Three rules make these work, and each exists because dropping it produced a real
failure in the run this sheet came from:

1. **One question per paste.** A compound question gets a merged answer, and a
   merged answer cannot be split back apart without guessing which half a
   sentence belongs to.
2. **Quotes before summaries, always.** A model asked to summarise first will
   then go hunting for quotes that fit the conclusion it already wrote. Every
   block below therefore asks for raw quotes and links *before* any summary.
3. **Ask about objections separately, and early.** Rounds that ask what a market
   wants return nothing about why it refuses. The objection has to be its own
   question or it never arrives.

Replace `<TOPIC>` with the subject, phrased the way the market phrases it.

## Q1 — objections

> When it comes to <TOPIC>, what are the words and phrases the critics and
> sceptics use? Who is arguing that it is a waste of money, theatre, or
> unnecessary complexity, and what exactly do they say? Give me their direct
> quotes and links first, before any summary.

## Q2 — price

> When it comes to <TOPIC>, what do people say about cost and price? What are
> they paying now, what do they say is too expensive, and what do they say is
> worth paying for? Give me direct quotes with prices and links first, before any summary.

## Q3 — rooms

> When it comes to <TOPIC>, what are the named communities where these people
> actually gather? Give me specific forums, servers and newsletters by name, and
> for each one tell me its posting rules on self-promotion and what it takes to
> be accepted there as a new member.

## Q4 — the literal ask

> When it comes to <TOPIC>, what exactly do people type when they are looking
> for a solution? Give me the verbatim sentences and questions people post when
> asking for a recommendation, with links, before any summary.

## Q5 — repellent language

> When it comes to <TOPIC>, what marketing language does this community mock or
> dismiss? What phrasing makes them distrust a product immediately? Give me
> direct quotes and links first, before any summary.

## Q6 — self-description

> When it comes to <TOPIC>, how do these people describe themselves and their
> own role, in their own words? Give me the direct quotes where they say what
> they are and what they do, with links, before any summary.

## What this sheet cannot get you

Run it and you still will not have: the reach behind any quote, whether the
speaker sells in the category, what the thread around the quote said, whether a
named product exists, or which rooms actually carry the topic. Those come from
opening things. The sheet is the input stage, not the research.
